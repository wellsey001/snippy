Find Shorts

Snippy listens to a long video (a podcast, stream, vlog or talk) and finds the moments that work on their own: a strong opening line, a finished thought, a lively voice. Each one becomes a portrait Short with captions, keeping the speaker in shot.

AutoCut

Pick your clips and Snippy edits them for you: it finds the best moments (skipping dark or blurry bits), cuts to the music or tidies up what's said, and makes a few versions to choose from.
